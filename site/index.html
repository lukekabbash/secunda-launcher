    How it works

    Your game thinks it's on a Windows PC. Each layer below does one job, and every one of them is open source. Pick a step to see what it does.

    1. Open Secunda

      Drag it to Applications and open it. The first time, it builds a private Windows space in your Library folder. If your Mac needs Rosetta, macOS offers to install it.

    2. Sign in to Steam

      Secunda downloads Steam straight from Valve. You sign in inside Steam itself, so Secunda never sees your password.

    3. Install and press Play

      Install a game through Steam, then press Play in Secunda. It writes that game's settings for your screen and hands it to Steam to start.

    What Secunda does for you

    • Fits your screen

      Reads your Mac's real display size and picks borderless, fullscreen or windowed per game, so the picture fills the screen and the HUD sits where it should.

    • Settings before you play

      Shadows, ambient occlusion, god rays, anti-aliasing, field of view and frame pacing, set from Secunda without editing INI files. Skyrim and Fallout 4 start from the game's own Medium preset.

    • Spoken dialogue works

      Bethesda games need Microsoft's audio components for spoken lines. Secunda fetches them from Microsoft on first Play and checks every file.

    • Knows your DLC

      Spots Dawnguard, Hearthfire, Dragonborn and the Anniversary content in Skyrim, and the Fallout 4 add-ons you own.

    • Stops cleanly

      One Stop button closes Steam and every game properly. If something hangs, Secunda can force it closed without touching your saves.

    • Updates itself

      Checks once a day and installs only updates signed by this project. If a game is running, it asks you before closing it.

    Open source, all the way down

    Every line of Secunda is on GitHub, and so is every piece it ships. It doesn't include the CrossOver app or any closed-source graphics layer. Each download carries the full source for its Windows layer, the build scripts and patches, every license, and a machine-readable list of components.

    Your account stays yours

    • No Steam password. You sign in inside Steam. Secunda never asks for it and never stores it.
    • No accounts, no analytics. Secunda talks only to Valve for Steam, Microsoft for game audio, and GitHub for updates.
    • Your files stay put. Games, saves and Steam live in one folder on your Mac. Updating or deleting the app leaves them alone.
    • Signed by the developer. Releases are signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
    • Nothing hidden. The code that runs on your Mac is the code on GitHub. You can read it or build it yourself.

    Questions

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Secunda is free and open source. You do need your own copy of each game on Steam.

    Which Macs does it work on?

    Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 15 Sequoia or newer. I've played every "Played" game on an M5 MacBook Air with 24 GB of memory. Other M-series Macs should work too; if yours has trouble, please open an issue with the diagnostics report from Settings.

    Does it work on Intel Macs?

    No. Secunda is built for Apple silicon only.

    Can I play DirectX 12 games?

    Not yet. Secunda handles DirectX 9, 10 and 11. The open-source projects it uses are working on DirectX 12, and Secunda will add it once it's dependable.

    What about online games and anti-cheat?

    Online modes that need kernel anti-cheat (Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, Vanguard) won't connect, because that software can't run on a Mac. Single-player and offline modes are often fine.

    Is this CrossOver or Whisky?

    No. Secunda is an independent project, not made by or affiliated with CodeWeavers or the Whisky project. It builds its own Wine from the source code CodeWeavers publishes and doesn't need the CrossOver app. It focuses on a short list of games that are set up properly.

    Where are my games and saves?

    In ~/Library/Application Support/Secunda Launcher. Updating Secunda never touches that folder, and deleting the app doesn't delete it either. Secunda can also back up your saves.

    How do I uninstall it?

    Quit Secunda and drag it to the Trash. To remove games, saves and Steam too, delete the folder above. Back up any saves you want to keep first.
