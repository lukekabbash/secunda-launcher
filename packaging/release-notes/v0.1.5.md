Secunda launches Windows Steam games I already own on Apple silicon. Steam handles sign-in and ownership. I never ask for a Steam password.

## 0.1.5

Skyrim Special Edition and Fallout 4 now start cleanly on a new Mac. Before the first launch, Secunda copies each game's shipped settings files into My Games with the Medium quality preset. Earlier builds wrote only display settings there, and Skyrim could close right after it opened.

If an earlier build left a display-only settings file, the next Play rebuilds it and keeps the display choices. Complete settings files stay as they are.

Native audio setup now checks every audio file it downloads before using it. A damaged install is repaired on the next Play.

## Compatibility

I have played these on my 2026 MacBook Air M5 with 24 GB of memory:

- The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition
- Fallout 4
- Supreme Commander 2
- Insurgency. A bit laggy.

Call of Duty: Black Ops II campaign exits early. Multiplayer and Zombies still need gameplay acceptance.

Experimental catalog titles: Supreme Commander, Supreme Commander: Forged Alliance, STAR WARS Battlefront II (Classic, 2005), Angels Fall First, Fallout: New Vegas, Portal 2, Half-Life 2, and Enderal Forgotten Stories SE.
