Secunda launches Windows Steam games I already own on Apple silicon. Steam handles sign-in and ownership. I never ask for a Steam password.

## 0.1.8

Skyrim no longer shows a dark patch on the ground that follows the camera and grows when you jump. Outdoor shadows from the world are now off by default, which is the only setting that removes it on a Mac today. Your other visual settings are untouched. Skyrim's page in Secunda still offers outdoor shadows at short, medium or long distance if you want them, but the patch comes back with them.

Secunda now keeps a small local log of how hard each running game works your Mac, so performance problems can be diagnosed from a real play session. It stays on your Mac.
