Secunda launches Windows Steam games I already own on Apple silicon. Steam handles sign-in and ownership. I never ask for a Steam password.

## 0.1.7

Black Ops II now launches fullscreen. Zombies, Multiplayer and Campaign open as a fullscreen window that covers the whole screen, and Windowed is still available on each mode's page. If you played Black Ops II on 0.1.6, which could only run windowed, your saved setting moves to fullscreen once; if you choose Windowed again afterwards, Secunda keeps it.
