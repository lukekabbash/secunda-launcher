# Secunda Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Secunda Launcher ("Secunda") is published by LK Digital Solutions LLC ("we"). This page explains what information Secunda touches. The short answer is: almost none, and none of it comes to us.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- We don't run any server that the app reports to. We never receive your Steam login, your library, your play time or your files.
- Everything Secunda saves stays on your Mac.
- The code is open, so anyone can check all of this.

## What stays on your Mac

Secunda keeps its settings, logs, a record of recent launches (used to tell you why a game didn't start) and its private Windows environment, including Steam and your installed games, in `~/Library/Application Support/Secunda Launcher`. None of it is uploaded by Secunda. Deleting that folder removes it.

## Connections the app makes

Secunda contacts other companies' servers only to do the following. Each of them sees your IP address and basic connection details, as any website would, and handles that under its own privacy policy.

| When | Who is contacted | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Once a day, and when you choose "Check for Updates" | GitHub | To see whether a new version of Secunda exists and to download it if you accept. Only a standard update request is sent, with no profile of your Mac. |
| When you set up Steam | Valve | To download the official Steam installer. |
| When your library is shown | Valve's image servers | To load cover images for your games. |
| When you choose to install voice audio for a game | Microsoft | To download Microsoft's official DirectX audio files. |

After that, Steam and your games make their own connections, just as they do on a Windows PC. What they collect is covered by Valve's and each publisher's privacy policies, not this one. Secunda doesn't read, intercept or copy that traffic.

## Diagnostics

Secunda can build a diagnostics report to help track down a problem. It is created only when you ask for it and saved on your Mac. It is never sent automatically. It can include your Mac model, macOS version, display details, game settings, file paths (with your home folder name stripped out) and recent logs. Game logs are written by the games themselves, so they can occasionally contain other details.

If you attach a report to a public GitHub issue, everyone can read it. Please look it over first.

## Microphone

Some games open the microphone for voice chat as soon as they start, so macOS may ask whether Secunda can use it. Secunda itself never records or listens. If you allow access, the audio goes only to the game you are running. You can say no, or change your mind later in System Settings.

## The website

The Secunda website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Like nearly all websites, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and reliability. Download links lead to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.

## Children

Secunda isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

## Your choices

Because we don't hold any information about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. Everything Secunda stores is on your Mac and under your control. If you post something to the project's GitHub pages and want it removed, you can delete it there or ask us to.

## Changes

If Secunda ever starts handling information differently, this page will change before the app does, and the date at the top will be updated. The full history of this policy is public in the project's repository.
